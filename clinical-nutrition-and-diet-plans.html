Clinical Nutrition & Diet Plans​

Clinical Nutrition & Diet Plans ​

At Impact Physiotherapy and Weight Loss Clinic, our nutrition approach goes beyond basic dieting. We focus on personalized, condition-based nutrition plans designed by experienced dietitians to support your health, metabolism, and long-term wellness.

Diabetes Diet Plan

A diabetes diet focuses on maintaining stable blood sugar levels through balanced nutrition and portion control. Managing diabetes is not just about avoiding sugar, but about choosing the right combination of foods at the right time.

At Impact Clinic, our dietitian creates customized meal plans based on your blood sugar levels, lifestyle, and medical history, helping you manage your condition effectively.

  • Helps control blood glucose levels
  • Improves energy and metabolism
  • Supports healthy weight management
  • Reduces risk of complications

Thyroid (Hypothyroidism) Diet

Thyroid imbalance can slow down metabolism, leading to weight gain, fatigue, and low energy levels. A well-planned diet plays a crucial role in managing thyroid function.

At Impact Clinic, we design nutrition plans that support metabolic balance and hormonal health, while also guiding you on foods to include and avoid.

  • Supports metabolism and energy levels
  • Helps manage weight gain
  • Improves overall hormonal balance
  • Reduces fatigue and sluggishness

PCOD / PCOS Diet

PCOD / PCOS is a hormonal condition that can lead to weight gain, irregular cycles, and insulin resistance. Diet plays a key role in managing symptoms and improving overall health.

Our experts at Impact Clinic provide structured diet plans that focus on improving insulin sensitivity and hormonal balance, along with sustainable lifestyle changes.

  • Helps regulate hormones
  • Supports weight loss and fat reduction
  • Improves insulin sensitivity
  • Enhances overall well-being

Pre-Pregnancy Nutrition

Pre-conception nutrition is essential to prepare the body for a healthy pregnancy. A balanced diet ensures that your body has the right nutrients to support both mother and baby.

At Impact Clinic, our dietitian guides you with personalized plans to improve your nutritional status, weight balance, and overall health before pregnancy.

  • Prepares body for healthy pregnancy
  • Improves nutritional balancen
  • Supports fertility and wellness
  • Builds strong foundation for maternal health

Post Pregnancy Diet

Post-pregnancy is a crucial phase where the body needs proper nutrition for recovery, energy, and overall well-being. A balanced diet helps in healing and managing weight safely.

At Impact Clinic, we provide personalized diet plans to support recovery, energy levels, and gradual weight loss, while ensuring proper nourishment for both mother and baby.

  • Supports post-delivery recovery
  • Improves energy and strength
  • Helps in safe weight loss
  • Ensures proper nutrition

Hypertension (High BP) Diet

High blood pressure can increase the risk of heart disease and stroke. A healthy diet plays a major role in controlling blood pressure levels.

At Impact Clinic, our dietitian designs heart-friendly meal plans focusing on low sodium, balanced nutrition, and healthy lifestyle habits.

  • Helps control blood pressure
  • Supports heart health
  • Improves overall fitness
  • Reduces health risks

Heart Health Diet

A heart-healthy diet focuses on improving cardiovascular health by managing cholesterol, blood pressure, and overall lifestyle.

At Impact Clinic, we guide you on choosing the right foods that support heart function and long-term health, along with practical lifestyle changes.

  • Improves heart function
  • Helps manage cholesterol levels
  • Reduces cardiovascular risks
  • Promotes long-term wellness

Muscle Gain Diet

Building lean muscle requires the right balance of nutrition and exercise. Proper intake of protein, calories, and nutrients is essential for muscle growth.

At Impact Clinic, we create customized diet plans that support muscle building, recovery, and performance, aligned with your fitness goals.

  • Supports lean muscle growth
  • Enhances recovery after workouts
  • Improves strength and endurance
  • Boosts metabolism

Skin, Hair and Nail Health Diet

Nutrition plays a key role in maintaining healthy skin, strong hair, and nails. A balanced diet rich in essential nutrients helps improve overall appearance and wellness.

At Impact Clinic, we guide you on nutrient-rich foods that support skin health, hair growth, and overall vitality.

  • Improves skin glow and texture
  • Strengthens hair and nails
  • Supports overall health
  • Enhances natural beauty
