Corporate Wellness Programs​

At Impact Physiotherapy and Weight Loss Clinic, we offer customized Corporate Wellness Programs designed to improve employee health, reduce workplace injuries, and enhance productivity.

In today’s work environment—long sitting hours, screen exposure, travel, and stress—employees are highly prone to back pain, neck pain, posture-related issues, and lifestyle disorders.

Our goal is to help organizations build a healthier, more active, and productive workforce.

Why Corporate Wellness Matters

  • Reduces employee absenteeism
  • Improves productivity and performance
  • Prevents workplace injuries
  • Enhances employee satisfaction & morale
  • Promotes a healthy work culture

Our Corporate Wellness Services:

Ergonomics & Posture Assessment

We evaluate workplace setup and employee posture to reduce strain and prevent injuries.

  • Workstation assessment
  • Posture correction techniques
  • Practical ergonomic modifications

Onsite & Offsite Physiotherapy Services

We provide physiotherapy support at your workplace or clinic.

  • Pain assessment & quick consultations
  • Supervised physiotherapy sessions
  • Injury management and rehabilitation

Corporate Workshops & Training Sessions

Interactive and engaging sessions designed for awareness and prevention.

  • Posture & ergonomics workshops
  • Back pain & neck pain prevention
  • Injury prevention sessions
  • Workplace stretching routines

Employee Fitness & Wellness Programs

Structured programs to improve overall fitness and reduce lifestyle-related issues.

  • Desk exercise routines
  • Stretching and mobility programs
  • Strength and posture correction exercises

Prevention & Early Intervention Programs

We focus on identifying risks early and preventing long-term health issues.

  • Musculoskeletal screening
  • Early pain management
  • Personalized exercise plans

Common Workplace Conditions We Address

  • Back & lower back pain
  • Neck & shoulder pain
  • Wrist & elbow issues (Carpal Tunnel Syndrome)
  • Postural dysfunction
  • Muscle stiffness & joint pain
  • Sciatica and nerve-related pain
  • Headaches, dizziness & fatigue

Our Approach

At Impact Clinic, we believe corporate wellness should be interactive, personalized, and result-driven.

  • Quick assessments & expert guidance
  • Customized programs based on company needs
  • Combination of physiotherapy, fitness & education
  • Continuous engagement and follow-ups
