Orthopedic Physiotherapy in Viman Nagar, Pune​

Orthopedic Physiotherapy​

Orthopedic conditions include problems related to bones, joints, muscles, ligaments, and cartilage. These conditions can cause pain, stiffness, reduced mobility, and impact daily activities.

At Impact Physiotherapy and Weight Loss Clinic, we provide advanced, evidence-based treatment for a wide range of orthopedic conditions, helping patients recover faster and regain strength and movement. Conditions we treat:

Back Pain Treatment

Back pain is one of the most common conditions, often caused by poor posture, prolonged sitting, muscle weakness, or spinal issues such as slip disc.

At Impact Clinic, we offer personalized physiotherapy programs to:

  • Reduce pain and stiffness
  • Improve posture and flexibility
  • Strengthen core and back muscles
  • Prevent recurrence of pain

Neck Pain Treatment

Neck pain can result from poor posture, long screen time, incorrect sleeping positions, or injury.

Our physiotherapy approach focuses on:

  • Pain relief and muscle relaxation
  • Improving neck mobility and flexibility
  • Posture correction and ergonomic advice
  • Strengthening and stretching exercises

Knee Pain Treatment

Knee pain may occur due to injury, arthritis, muscle imbalance, or age-related wear and tear.

We provide targeted physiotherapy treatment to:

  • Reduce pain and inflammation
  • Improve joint mobility and functions
  • Strengthen surrounding muscles

Support faster recovery after injury or surgery

Acute and Chronic Pain Treatment

Acute pain is short-term pain caused by injury, strain, or sudden trauma.

Chronic pain persists for a longer duration and may affect daily activities and quality of life.

At Impact Physiotherapy Clinic, we provide effective pain management in Pune using a holistic approach that includes:

  • Pain relief therapies (Manual Therapy, Electrotherapy)
  • Assisted stretching and mobility exercises
  • Strengthening and rehabilitation programs
  • Long-term pain management strategies

We treat conditions such as neck pain, back pain, joint pain, and chronic musculoskeletal pain, helping patients recover and regain function.

Ankle and Foot Pain Treatment

Foot and ankle pain can significantly affect your ability to walk, stand, and perform daily activities. These conditions may arise due to injury, overuse, improper footwear, or structural issues.

Common symptoms include pain, swelling, stiffness, difficulty in walking, and discomfort during movement.

At Impact Clinic, we offer targeted physiotherapy to:

  • Reduce pain and swelling
  • Improve mobility and flexibility
  • Strengthen muscles and support joints
  • Restore normal walking and movement

Our goal is to help you return to pain-free mobility and daily function.

Arthritis & Rheumatoid Arthritis Treatment

Arthritis is a condition that causes pain, stiffness, and inflammation in the joints, commonly affecting the knees, back, and neck. The most common types include Osteoarthritis and Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA).

Physiotherapy plays a key role in managing arthritis by improving joint function and reducing pain.

At Impact Clinic, our treatment focuses on:

  • Reducing joint pain and stiffness
  • Improving flexibility and range of motion
  • Strengthening muscles around the joints
  • Enhancing overall mobility and function

Early physiotherapy intervention can help slow progression and improve quality of life.

Shoulder Pain Treatment

Shoulder pain is a common musculoskeletal condition caused by injury, poor posture, muscle imbalance, or post-surgical stiffness. The shoulder is a complex joint, and even minor issues can affect movement and daily activities.

At Impact Physiotherapy Clinic, we provide targeted treatment to:

  • Reduce pain and inflammation
  • Restore shoulder mobility and function
  • Improve strength and stability
  • Support recovery after injury or surgery

Our goal is to help you regain pain-free movement and normal shoulder function.

Sciatica Treatment

Sciatica causes pain that radiates from the lower back to the leg due to nerve compression or irritation.

Our physiotherapy approach focuses on:

  • Reducing nerve inflammation and pain
  • Relieving muscle tightness
  • Improving spinal mobility
  • Preventing recurrence

We use safe, non-invasive techniques to help you return to daily activities without discomfort.

Ligament Injury Rehabilitation

Ligament injuries commonly affect the knee, ankle, and shoulder, often due to sports injuries or sudden movements.

At Impact Clinic, our rehabilitation programs help to:

  • Reduce pain and swelling
  • Stabilize the affected joint
  • Restore strength and mobility
  • Prevent re-injury

Our focus is on complete recovery and return to normal activity levels

Post-Amputation Rehabilitation

Limb loss can significantly impact mobility and independence. Our specialized physiotherapy programs are designed to support recovery and improve quality of life.

We provide rehabilitation for:

  • Above/Below Knee Amputation
  • Above Elbow & Forearm Amputation
  • Other Limb Loss Conditions
