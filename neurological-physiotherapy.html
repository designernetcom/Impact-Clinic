Neurological Physiotherapy in Viman Nagar, Pune

Neurological Physiotherapy​

Neurological physiotherapy focuses on treating conditions affecting the brain, spinal cord, and nerves, such as stroke, Parkinson’s disease, multiple sclerosis, and spinal cord injuries.

At Impact Physiotherapy and Weight Loss Clinic, we provide specialized neuro rehabilitation in Pune, designed to improve mobility, strength, balance, and overall independence.

Our goal is to help patients regain function, restore movement, and improve quality of life through personalized, evidence-based treatment.

Stroke Rehabilitation

Stroke (Cerebrovascular Accident) occurs due to reduced blood flow to the brain, leading to loss of movement and function.

Our physiotherapy focuses on:

  • Regaining movement and strength
  • Improving balance and coordination
  • Relearning daily activities (walking, sitting, standing)
  • Enhancing independence and quality of life

Early rehabilitation plays a key role in faster recovery.

Diabetic Neuropathy Treatment

Diabetic neuropathy is nerve damage caused by diabetes, commonly affecting the legs and feet, leading to pain, numbness, and weakness.

Our treatment helps to:

  • Reduce pain and tingling sensations
  • Improve circulation and nerve function
  • Strengthen muscles
  • Enhance mobility and balance

Parkinson’s disease physiotherapy

Parkinson’s disease is a neurodegenerative condition affecting movement, balance, and coordination.

Our physiotherapy program focuses on:

  • Improving mobility and flexibility
  • Enhancing balance and posture
  • Reducing stiffness and muscle tightness
  • Maintaining functional independence

Facial (Bells) Palsy Treatment

Facial palsy occurs due to nerve inflammation affecting facial muscles, leading to weakness or loss of movement.

Our physiotherapy helps to:

  • Improve facial muscle strength
  • Restore facial expressions
  • Enhance coordination of facial movements
  • Support recovery through guided exercises

Spinal Cord Injury

Spinal cord injuries can lead to loss of movement, sensation, and functional ability, depending on severity.

Our rehabilitation program includes:

  • Mobility and strength training
  • Balance and coordination exercises
  • Functional independence training
  • Long-term recovery support

Guillain-Barre Syndrome (GBS)

Guillain-Barre Syndrome (GBS) is a rare condition where the immune system affects the nerves, causing weakness and reduced sensation.

Our physiotherapy focuses on:

  • Restoring muscle strength
  • Improving mobility and function
  • Gradual recovery through personalized programs

Multiple sclerosis or MS

Multiple sclerosis or MS Multiple sclerosis affects the central nervous system, leading to issues with movement, balance, and coordination.

Our Physiotherapy helps to:

  • Improve mobility and muscle control
  • Manage fatigue and muscle stiffness
  • Enhance balance and coordination
  • Maintain independence in daily activities

Cerebral Palsy Physiotherapy

Cerebral palsy affects movement, muscle tone, and posture, usually from early childhood.

Our Physiotherapist helps to:

  • Improve movement and coordination
  • Enhance muscle strength
  • Support posture and balance
  • Improve functional independence

Balance & Vestibular Rehabilitation

Balance disorders and dizziness can occur due to inner ear or neurological issues.

Our treatment focuses on:

  • Improving balance and stability
  • Reducing dizziness and fall risk
  • Enhancing coordination
  • Restoring confidence in movement
