Advanced Physiotherapy Technologies & Modalities in Viman Nagar, Pune​

At Impact Physiotherapy and Weight Loss Clinic, we use cutting-edge, non-invasive therapies to reduce pain, accelerate healing, and improve recovery outcomes alongside manual therapy and exercise rehabilitation.

💠
Matrix Therapy

An advanced technique working at a cellular level to improve muscle function and circulation. Stimulates tissues and restores normal muscle activity.

  • Improves blood circulation and oxygen supply
  • Reduces muscle stiffness and pain
  • Enhances tissue healing
  • Restores normal muscle function
Effective For

Chronic pain, muscle stiffness, sports injuries, post-injury recovery

🔆
Laser Therapy

Uses light energy to reduce pain and inflammation while promoting tissue repair at a cellular level. Safe, painless, and effective.

  • Reduces pain and inflammation
  • Accelerates tissue healing
  • Improves circulation
  • Non-invasive and comfortable
Effective For

Joint pain, muscle injuries, inflammation, post-injury recovery

⚡
Shockwave Therapy

A modern, non-invasive treatment using acoustic waves to stimulate healing in damaged tissues. Highly effective for stubborn, long-standing pain.

  • Breaks down scar tissue and calcification
  • Promotes faster tissue healing
  • Reduces chronic pain
  • Improves mobility and function
Effective For

Plantar fasciitis, tennis elbow, shoulder pain, chronic tendon injuries

📍
Dry Needling Therapy

Targets tight muscle bands and trigger points to release tension, improve circulation, and accelerate recovery from soft tissue injuries.

  • Relieves muscle tightness and pain
  • Improves blood circulation
  • Enhances mobility and flexibility
  • Speeds up natural healing
Effective For

Neck pain, back pain, muscle spasm, sports injuries, chronic pain

🏺
Cupping Therapy

Uses suction cups to improve blood circulation, reduce muscle tension, and promote natural healing as part of a comprehensive treatment plan.

  • Reduces muscle tightness and stiffness
  • Improves blood circulation
  • Promotes faster healing
  • Helps in pain relief and relaxation
Effective For

Back pain, neck pain, sports injuries, muscle recovery

🧲
Magnet Therapy

Uses magnetic fields combined with heat to improve blood flow, reduce inflammation, and promote healing as a supportive treatment.

  • Reduces pain and inflammation
  • Improves circulation
  • Relaxes muscles and joints
  • Supports faster healing
Effective For

Joint pain, arthritis, back pain, muscle stiffness

🔊
Ultrasound Therapy

Uses sound waves to promote tissue healing and reduce inflammation at a cellular level for deep recovery.

  • Reduces pain and swelling
  • Promotes tissue repair
  • Improves blood circulation
Effective For

Ligament injuries, tendon issues, muscle pain

〰️
IFT (Interferential Therapy)

Advanced electrotherapy for deep pain relief without discomfort on the skin surface.

  • Reduces chronic and acute pain
  • Improves blood flow
  • Promotes healing
Effective For

Back pain, joint pain, post-surgical recovery

⚡
TENS Therapy

Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation relieves pain by stimulating sensory nerves a drug-free solution.

  • Drug-free pain relief
  • Safe and non-invasive
  • Suitable for acute & chronic pain
Effective For

Drug-free relief from acute and chronic pain conditions

💪
Electrical Muscle Stimulation (EMS)

Stimulates muscles using electrical impulses to improve strength, reduce stiffness, and support neurological rehabilitation.

  • Strengthens weak muscles
  • Reduces muscle stiffness
  • Supports neurological rehabilitation
Effective For

Muscle strengthening, rehabilitation, improving muscle function

🪜
Traction Therapy

Gently stretches the spine to reduce pressure on discs and nerves, improving alignment and relieving compression.

  • Relieves back and neck pain
  • Reduces nerve compression
  • Improves spinal alignment
Effective For

Slip disc, sciatica, cervical & lumbar spondylosis

🌡️
Short Wave Diathermy (SWD)

Uses electromagnetic waves to provide deep tissue heating for pain relief and improved flexibility.

  • Relieves muscle and joint pain
  • Improves circulation
  • Reduces stiffness
Effective For

Back pain, knee pain, and chronic conditions
