About Dr. Naziya Bagwan – Expert Physiotherapist in Viman Nagar, Pune

Dr. Naziya Bagwan is the Founder of Impact Physiotherapy and Weight Loss Clinic in Viman Nagar, Pune. With over 17+ years of experience in physiotherapy, she is known for delivering effective, result-oriented treatment for a wide range of conditions.

She has successfully treated thousands of patients across OPD and ICU settings, including complex orthopedic and neurological cases, during her tenure at reputed hospitals in Mumbai and Pune.

Dr. Naziya offers advanced physiotherapy care for:

  • Orthopedic Conditions: Back Pain, Neck Pain, Slip Disc, Knee Pain, Joint Pain, Arthritis
  • Neurological Rehabilitation: Stroke, Paralysis, Parkinson’s Disease, Nerve Disorders
  • Sports Injury Rehabilitation: Ligament Injuries, Muscle Tears
  • Pre & Post-Operative Rehab: ACL, Joint Replacement, Fractures
  • Cardiac & Respiratory Rehabilitation
  • Electrotherapy & Pain Management
  • Exercise Therapy & Functional Training
  • Weight Loss & Fitness Programs

Dr. Naziya strongly believes that accurate diagnosis and personalized treatment are the keys to faster and long-lasting recovery. She focuses on delivering realistic expectations and measurable results, making her a trusted physiotherapist in Pune. Her approach includes:

  • Accurate Diagnosis (Root cause approach)
  • Personalised Treatment Plans
  • Combination of Manual Therapy and Exercise Rehab
  • Focus on long-term recovery (Not temporary relief)

Apart from clinical practice, Dr. Naziya actively conducts corporate health and wellness programs, including:

  • Corporate health and wellness sessions
  • Posture correction workshops
  • Ergonomics training for office professionals
  • Workplace injury prevention programs

These programs help prevent lifestyle-related issues and improve workplace productivity.
