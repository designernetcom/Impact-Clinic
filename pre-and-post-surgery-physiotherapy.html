Pre and Post-Surgery Physiotherapy​

Pre and post-operative rehabilitation is essential for a faster, safer, and more effective recovery after surgery.

At Impact Physiotherapy and Weight Loss Clinic, we provide personalized rehabilitation programs designed to improve strength, mobility, and overall function — both before and after surgery.

Our goal is to help you recover faster, reduce complications, and return to normal life confidently.

Pre-Operative Rehabilitation (Pre-Surgery Physio)

Pre-operative physiotherapy prepares your body for surgery and improves post-surgical outcomes.

  • Reduces pain and inflammation
  • Improves joint mobility and flexibility
  • Builds strength and stamina
  • Prepares the body for faster recovery

A structured prehab program ensures you are physically ready for surgery and recover better post-surgery.

Post – Operative Rehabilitation

Post-surgery physiotherapy is crucial to regain movement, strength, and function safely.

  • Reduces pain, swelling, and stiffness
  • Restores joint mobility and flexibility
  • Improves muscle strength
  • Helps return to daily activities faster

Our physiotherapists design customized recovery plans based on your surgery and condition.

Structured Rehabilitation Program

A structured and prescribed exercise program helps to restore range of movement, decrease pain, improve muscle strength and mobility, and get you back doing the things you love more quickly. Every patient receives a personalized and goal-based rehab plan, which includes:

  • Manual Therapy
  • Guided Exercise Programs
  • Electrotherapy & Advanced Modalities
  • Mobility & Strength Training
  • Home Exercise Guidance

This ensures safe recovery, reduced complications, and long-term results

We provide rehabilitation for surgeries

We provide comprehensive rehabilitation support across a wide range of surgical procedures, ensuring safe and effective recovery at every stage. Our programs are tailored to help patients regain strength, mobility, and confidence post-surgery.

  • Knee Replacement (TKR)
  • Hip Replacement
  • ACL Reconstruction
  • Fracture Surgeries
  • Spine Surgeries (Cervical/Lumbar)
  • Shoulder Surgeries

Our Approach

Our rehabilitation approach is personalized, goal-oriented, and focused on long-term recovery. We combine advanced physiotherapy techniques with expert care to deliver the best possible outcomes for every patient.

  • Early mobilization
  • Pain management
  • Functional recovery
  • Patient education
