About Impact Physiotherapy & Weight Loss Clinic in Viman Nagar, Pune

Impact Physiotherapy and Weight Loss Clinic, founded by Dr. Naziya Bagwan in 2022, is a leading center for pain relief, rehabilitation, and fitness transformation in Viman Nagar, Pune.

We are committed to delivering prompt, effective, and evidence-based physiotherapy care, helping patients recover faster and achieve long-term results. Our expertise covers treating wide range of conditions including:

  • Musculoskeletal and Orthopedic conditions
  • Neurological disorder
  • Patient Centered Approach
  • Sports injuries and Rehabilitation
  • Cardiac and Respiratory conditions

At Impact Clinic, we combine advanced physiotherapy techniques with a personalized approach. Every patient receives a customized treatment plan based on their condition, lifestyle, and recovery goals. Our approach integrates advanced techniques such as

  • Manual Therapy
  • Electrotherapy
  • Exercise Eehabilitation
  • Functional Training and Fitness programs

We provide effective solutions for acute, sub-acute, and chronic conditions, focusing on long-terms recovery and improved quality of life.
